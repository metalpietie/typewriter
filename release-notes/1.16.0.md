## Version 1.16.0: statistics for a book, a project, or all projects

### New
- **Statistics for all projects.** A switch at the top of **Statistics** picks what the numbers count:
  - **This book:** one book of the open project. Pick the book under the switch. It shows when the project has 2 or more books.
  - **This project:** all books of the open project, as before.
  - **All projects:** every project on the bookshelf, also the books in a shelf group. The words per day of all projects are added up. **Words per book** lists every book, by project.
- **Words per day, per book.** The Write tab now also saves which book the words go to, so **This book** can count them.

### Notes
- Your old statistics stay. **This project** and **All projects** show all your old days.
- **This book** only counts words written from this version on. Before, the app kept one count per day for the whole project, so older words cannot be split by book.
- In **All projects**, the daily goal is the goal of the open project. Each project keeps its own goal.
- The build tool (Vite) is updated to fix 3 security alerts. These were in a tool for building the app, not in the app itself.
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.8.0.

## Version 1.17.1: round dots, neater buttons, and a blue phone app

### Changed
- **The phone app is blue too.** Typewriter Notes for Android (1.9.0) now uses the same blue and navy as Typewriter and its icon, instead of purple, in the dark and the light theme. Download `typewriter-notes-1.17.1.apk` below and install it over the old one. Your notes stay.

### Fixed
- **Colour dots are fully round** everywhere: in the lists, on the Board, in the scene and chapter panels, on name cards, and next to the save state. They have a size in whole pixels, so no edge looks flat.
- **The people in a chapter** (and the factions of a character, and the people of a timeline event) sit neatly in their pill: the colour dot is as far from the left edge as from the top and the bottom.
- **⧉ New window** at the bottom of the menu fits on one line. It was called "This screen in a new window", and the text broke in the middle of a word.
- **Add portrait** (and **Add picture** of a place) looks like a button, and sits centred under the picture.
- In the spelling menu (right-click a red word), the suggestions no longer sit on top of each other.

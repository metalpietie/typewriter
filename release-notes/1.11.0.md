## Version 1.11.0: updates and an installer

### New
- **Updates from GitHub.** Typewriter can check for a new version and update itself. Use **Check for updates** next to the version at the bottom of the menu, or under **Updates** in Export & Backup. A window shows what is new, with **Update now**, **Later** and **Skip this version**.
- **Check when the app starts.** On at first, and once a day while the app stays open. Switch it off under **Updates** in Export & Backup. Only the newest version number is asked for: nothing of your writing is sent.
- **Safe updates.** Each update is checked against its checksum and a signature before anything changes. A file that does not match is thrown away. Before the restart, the app saves everything and keeps a copy of your data (`before-update-….db`).
- **A Windows installer.** It installs for you only, with no admin prompt, adds a Start menu entry and (if you want) a desktop shortcut, and an entry in Settings > Apps. Uninstalling keeps your writing and your settings.
- **Portable.** The plain `.exe` is the same program, without an installer.

### Notes
- The installer is not signed with a bought certificate, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- This is the first version that can update itself. Earlier versions must be replaced by hand once (run the installer, or use the new `.exe`).
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.8.0.

## Version 1.13.0: name cards

### New
- **Name cards.** Point at an @ link in your text for a moment. A small card shows who or what it is: the colour, name, role or type, and a short description. **Open** opens it, **Show beside** shows it in the reference pane. A click still only moves the cursor, so writing is not interrupted. It works for characters, places, lore notes, factions and items.

### Fixed
- When you open a scene on the Write page or a page on the Notes page, the cursor is now in the text. You can type, or use the Vim keys, at once, with no click first. It does not take the cursor away from a title field, the search box or an open window.

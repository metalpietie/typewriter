## Version 1.14.0: @ for non-fiction

### New
- **Cite with @.** Type **@** and the start of an author or a title. Your sources show in the list, marked **cite**. Pick one, and a citation goes into the text, the same as with the **Cite** button. Click the citation to add a page.
- **Glossary words with @.** Glossary words show in the @ list too. They go into the text as a link. Point at the link, and the name card shows the meaning. **Open** goes to the word in the Glossary.

### Fixed
- In a non-fiction project, typing **@** did nothing, because there was nothing to link to. Now it offers your glossary words and sources.
- On the name card, **Show beside** now only shows for characters, places and lore notes. Before, it also showed for factions and items, but the reference pane cannot show them, so it did nothing.

### Notes
- The @ list only offers things from the tools that are on in the project.
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.8.0.

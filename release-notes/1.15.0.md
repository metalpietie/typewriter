## Version 1.15.0: shelf groups, copy to another shelf, and a blue look

### New
- **Shelf groups: each book its own world.** A group is one shelf on the bookshelf with several books. Each book in a group has its own characters, places, notes and maps. Use this for books that do not share a world, such as practice novels.
  - On a shelf, click **Give each book its own world…** to turn the shelf into a group. The first book keeps the world. The other books each get a new, empty world.
  - Click **+** in a group to add a new book with its own world.
  - Rename a group by clicking its name. **Ungroup** makes each world a shelf of its own again.
  - In **Manage shelves…**, pick a group for a project, or type a name to make a new group.
- **Drag in a group.**
  - Drop a book in a gap to move it there. If it comes from another shelf, it gets its own new world.
  - Drop a book on another book to add it to that book's world. The app asks first.
  - A placeholder shows where the book came from, so you can put it back.
- **Copy to another shelf.** Click **Copy to…** on a character, place, lore note, faction, item, glossary word, source or Notes page. Pick the items and the shelf to copy to. The originals stay where they are.
  - Relationships, faction members, mood board pictures, @ links and citations come along when both ends are copied.
  - Maps and the timeline are not copied.

### Changed
- **Blue look.** The app now uses the blue of the icon, with a navy background, instead of purple. This is in the dark and the light theme.

### Fixed
- **Vim label.** With Vim keys on, the mode label (NORMAL, INSERT, VISUAL) was hidden behind the word count bar on the Write page. Now it always shows above the bar, wherever you are in the text.
- On a normal shelf, the drop placeholder was sometimes one place off when you dragged a book.

### Notes
- Groups are only a name in the list of projects. Your data files do not change.
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.8.0.

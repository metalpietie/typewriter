## Version 1.17.2: the cursor stays where you left it

### New
- **The cursor stays where you left it.** When you go back to a scene or a Notes page, the cursor is where it was the last time, and the page scrolls so that line is a little above the middle of the screen. You can type at once (and with Vim keys, move at once), with no scrolling to find your place in a long scene. A scene you never opened starts with the cursor at the end of its text. The places are kept on this computer, for the last 500 texts you opened.

### Notes
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.9.0.

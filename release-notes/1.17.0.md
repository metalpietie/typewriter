## Version 1.17.0: spell check, command menu, own fields, submission format, and a second window

### New
- **Spell check that knows your names.** A wavy red line marks a word that is not in the dictionary. Your characters, places, factions, items, glossary words, lore notes and book titles are known words, and @ links are never marked. Choose **English (UK)**, **English (US)** or **Off** under **Look**. It works offline.
  - Right-click a marked word for suggestions, **Add to the dictionary** or **Ignore it for now**.
  - The toolbar shows how many words are marked. Click it, or press **F7**, to go to the next one (**Shift+F7** for the one before).
  - With Vim keys: **]s** and **[s** go to the next and previous marked word, and **z=** shows the suggestions (press **1** to **5** to pick one).
  - **Checks → Spelling** lists every marked word of the book, with where it is. **Change all** fixes a word everywhere.
- **Command menu.** Press **F1** or **Ctrl+Shift+P**, or type **>** in the search box. Type a few letters ("new char", "go map", "split") and press Enter: make new things, use the Write tools, switch Vim keys or typewriter mode, go to any screen, and more.
- **Your own fields** on places, factions and items, not only on characters. Press **☆** on a field to give every character (or place, faction, item) that field. Your fields are found by search, show in the reference pane, and are in the story bible export.
- **Lore templates.** **New from a template…** on the Lore notes list makes a note with headings and questions to answer: magic system, religion, culture, kingdom, historical event, creature, invention, language, and economy.
- **Submission format.** **Submission** on the Export screen makes one book in standard manuscript format, as Word or PDF: a title page with your contact details and the word count, 12 pt Times, double spaced, *Surname / TITLE / page* at the top of each page, chapters a third of the way down a new page, # between scenes, and THE END.
- **A second window.** **⧉ This screen in a new window** at the bottom of the menu opens the screen you are on in its own window, for example the map on a second screen. The **⧉** button of the reference pane moves the reference into its own window. The windows keep each other up to date, and closing the main window saves and closes them all.

### Notes
- The spell check dictionaries are the open Hunspell lists (the ones LibreOffice uses). The words you add are kept on this computer.
- The Android app is unchanged and stays at 1.8.0.
